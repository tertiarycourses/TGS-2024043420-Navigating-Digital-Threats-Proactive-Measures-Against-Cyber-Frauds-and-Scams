## About the course

Learners analyse phishing, impersonation, investment, job, e-commerce, malware-enabled and business-email-compromise scam pathways. The activities build evidence-led verification, account and payment safeguards, incident evidence and escalation decisions.

## Learning outcomes

- Identify potential cyber security risks and threats, including internet frauds and scams.
- Protect personal and business information from internet frauds and scams.
- Interpret and escalate cyber frauds and scams to relevant authorities.

## Topics covered

- Scam attack chains, contact methods and observable signals
- Phishing URLs, email authentication and independent verification
- Device, account, identity and payment controls
- ScamShield checking and reporting
- Incident containment, evidence preservation and escalation
